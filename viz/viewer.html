Active, risk-aware multimodal search: simulation replay

Illustrative episodes from the released simulation (assumed sensor models, not hardware). Episodes were chosen to show differences between policies and are not typical; the paper reports averages over 300 episodes per scenario.

Pink dot: robot. Grey: visited and rejected. Green: confirmed victim. Red: false alarm. Yellow: being sampled. Outline markers show hidden truth: ★ victim, ▲ hazard, ◆ distractor, □ obstacle.
